## Executive conclusions (Investigation stop gate)

### Q1. Is 905.7 Hz a wrong F0?

**Partially — not the simple “3/4 masters wrong at 905.7” story.**

| master | clean F0 (autocorr / FFT peak) | harmonic F0 (autocorr / FFT peak) |
|--------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| final_01 | 905.7 / 900 | 905.7 / 900 |
| final_02 | **695.7 / 699** | **350.4 / 699** |
| final_03 | 905.7 / 900 | 905.7 / 900 |
| final_04 | 905.7 / 900 | 905.7 / 900 |

- Masters **01/03/04** are SMARD `sinus_tones_48kHz` clips tagged `f0_band=400-1000`. Autocorrelation, harmonic-tool F0, and FFT peak (~900 Hz) **agree within one FFT bin**. ACF confidence ≈ **0.999**; top lag is **53 → 905.66 Hz**, not a weak boundary tie-break.
- **HYP-1 (905.7 = fmax artifact)** is **not supported** for 01/03/04: the peak is interior to [50, 1000] Hz with strong confidence and matching FFT peak.
- **Master_02 harmonic** is different: autocorr **drops to 350.4 Hz** (subharmonic of ~700 Hz) while **FFT dominant energy stays at 699 Hz**. That is a **confirmed F0-estimator failure on the distorted waveform**, not on the clean master (695.7 Hz).

**Implication:** Fixing autocorrelation alone may help master_02 harmonic/combined paths, but **does not explain** why harmonic injection on 01/03/04 leaves THD ≈ 0.01% at the (likely correct) ~906 Hz fundamental.

### Q2. Source of THD ≈ 3.47×10⁶ % on master_02 harmonic?

**Confirmed: denominator pathology from F0 mis-association, not physics.**

At tool-estimated F0 = **350.4 Hz**:

- `h1_amplitude` = **9.05×10⁻⁷** at 354 Hz (negligible energy)
- `h2_amplitude` = **0.0306** at **699 Hz** (actual dominant line ≈ true fundamental)
- `h2_relative` = **33,813** → THD ≈ **3.38×10⁶ %**

`fundamental_amplitude` passes the current `eps` guard (9e-7 ≫ machine epsilon), so the tool returns **`valid: true`** despite an **unreliable fundamental bin**. This is **not** a true harmonic diagnosis; it is a **rule FAIL triggered by numerical pathology** when F0 and spectral energy diverge.

**HYP-2 confirmed.** Not “h1 exactly zero”, but **h1 at the wrong frequency is orders of magnitude below the harmonic being measured**.

### Q3. Transform vs detector semantic consistency?

**Yes on ideal signals; diverges on complex SMARD windows.**

With transform **1.1.0**, alpha=**0.50**, post_gain=**0.8**:

- Pure sine (440 / 350 / 906 Hz): injected THD ≈ **25–27%**, h2 ≈ **0.25** with correct F0 — **well above 5% demo rule**.
- Low-peak sine (peak=0.012): same **~25% THD** — amplitude alone is **not** the blocker.
- Real SMARD harmonic masters 01/03/04: THD remains **0.01–0.02%** despite injection — injection energy is **not associating at 2×F0** under the current harmonic association model (multi-tone / x² spread / intermodulation not captured as order-2 at 2×F0).

**Task 6 stop condition “THD < 5% at correct F0” applies to study design on real SMARD material, not to transform math on sines.**

**Revised verdict: RC-1 is only partially upheld.**

| Sub-claim | Verdict |

| “905.7 Hz is systematically wrong on 3/4 SMARD masters” | **Not confirmed** for clean 01/03/04 (consistent with material metadata and FFT) |
| “Wrong F0 causes harmonic FN because detector searches 2×905.7” | **Partially true** for 01/03/04: detector uses ~906 Hz but **injected h2 is not visible** there (THD ≈ 0) |
